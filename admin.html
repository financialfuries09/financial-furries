🦊 Financial Furries Guidelines

Keep articles educational, factual, clear about risks, and properly sourced.

🟢 DO

  • Explain concepts simply.
  • Use factual information and sources.
  • Explain risks and potential benefits.
  • Use dated historical data.
  • Label examples and opinions.

🔴 DON'T

  • Promise or guarantee returns.
  • Give personal buy/sell instructions.
  • Present predictions as certainty.
  • Hide important risks or fees.
  • Give personalized financial, tax, or legal advice.

Disclaimer: Information published by Financial Furries is for educational and informational purposes only and is not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Financial markets involve risk, and past performance does not guarantee future results.
