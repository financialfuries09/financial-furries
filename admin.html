🦊 Financial Furries Guidelines

Use these rules whenever you publish financial information. Keep articles educational, factual, and clear about risks.

🟢 DO

  • Explain financial concepts in simple language.
  • Use factual information and sources.
  • Explain risks as well as potential benefits.
  • Use historical data with dates and context.
  • Clearly label examples and opinions.

🔴 DON'T

  • Promise or guarantee returns.
  • Tell readers personally to buy or sell an investment.
  • Present predictions as certainty.
  • Hide important risks, fees, or limitations.
  • Give personalized financial, tax, or legal advice.
